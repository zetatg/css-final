Wonders of China

Five thousand years of history, culture, and natural splendour

Welcome

China is one of the world's oldest continuous civilisations, stretching across vast landscapes from the Gobi Desert to the tropical shores of Hainan. From ancient imperial palaces to towering sandstone pillars cloaked in mist, every corner of this remarkable country holds a story.

This site celebrates five iconic wonders — places that capture the extraordinary beauty and depth of Chinese heritage. Browse the Gallery to see stunning imagery, or head to Explore for travel highlights and essential facts.

Five Wonders at a Glance

The Great Wall

A 21,000 km fortification built over centuries to protect the northern frontier of China.

The Forbidden City

Beijing's imperial palace complex served as the home of 24 emperors across 500 years.

Zhangjiajie

Towering quartzite sandstone pillars draped in subtropical forest — the inspiration for Avatar's Hallelujah Mountains.

Li River & Guilin

The meandering Li River winds between dramatic karst peaks, one of China's most iconic landscapes.

Terracotta Army

Discovered in 1974, over 8,000 life-sized clay soldiers guard the tomb of Emperor Qin Shi Huang.

West Lake, Hangzhou

A UNESCO World Heritage landscape of temples, pagodas, and causeways that inspired centuries of poetry.
