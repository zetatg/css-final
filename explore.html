Destination Highlights

The Great Wall of China

Construction of the wall began as early as the 7th century BC. The most famous sections near Beijing — Badaling, Mutianyu, and Jinshanling — were built during the Ming Dynasty (1368–1644). Visiting at dawn, before the crowds arrive, is one of China's most unforgettable experiences.

  • Location: Northern China, from Shanhaiguan to Jiayuguan Pass
  • Best time to visit: April–May or September–November
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1987

The Forbidden City, Beijing

Covering 72 hectares and containing over 9,000 rooms, the Forbidden City is the world's largest preserved ancient wooden palace complex. Completed in 1420, it served as the ceremonial and political heart of Chinese government for nearly 500 years. Today it is home to the Palace Museum with a collection of over a million artefacts.

  • Location: Tiananmen Square, Beijing
  • Best time to visit: Weekday mornings in spring or autumn
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1987

Zhangjiajie National Forest Park

Established in 1982 as China's first national forest park, Zhangjiajie is famous for its more than 3,000 quartzite sandstone pillars — the tallest of which rises 1,080 metres. The Bailong Elevator, the world's tallest outdoor lift, carries visitors from the valley floor to viewing platforms at cliff level.

  • Location: Hunan Province, central China
  • Best time to visit: March–May or October–November
  • UNESCO World Heritage Site (part of Wulingyuan) since 1992

Essential Travel Facts

  • Currency: Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB). Most cities accept mobile payments (WeChat Pay, Alipay).
  • Language: Mandarin Chinese (Putonghua) is the official language; English is widely spoken in tourist areas.
  • Visa: Most nationalities require a visa; China introduced a 144-hour transit visa-free policy for many countries.
  • Getting around: China's high-speed rail network (HSR) is the largest in the world — fast, affordable, and punctual.
  • Time zone: All of China operates on a single time zone: China Standard Time (CST / UTC+8).
